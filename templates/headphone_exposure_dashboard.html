About the data

Recorded minutes count distinct clock minutes with at least one log entry. Exact active seconds are not stored, so this is an activity footprint, not precise listening duration. Missing records can mean silence, capture downtime, or that monitoring was off.

The level is the recorder’s uncalibrated upper estimate, not a measurement at the eardrum. Average estimates combine sound energy, with equal weight per recorded minute. If a minute has multiple entries, their energies receive equal weight within that minute because durations are unavailable. “Highest minute” is the highest combined minute average, not an instantaneous peak.

Weekly dose is the latest cumulative percentage saved by the recorder for the displayed week. Dose increases are positive differences between consecutive readings within the same Monday–Sunday week. The first reading is a baseline; a decrease starts a new baseline. Differences across gaps are assigned to the later reading, so daily attribution can be approximate. No missing time is inferred.

Dates and hours use each record’s saved time zone. Range buttons end at the latest recorded date. Weekly bars show only available days in the selected range. Missing days are excluded from averages. Today and weeks with fewer than seven recorded dates are marked as partial.
